GGROBAR

It's Christmas Eve, and Grandma's walking home from the party. Help her through town, over the sledding hill, across the frozen pond and through the woods.

Watch for the red glow. When you hear sleigh bells and see a red light at the side of a row, the sleigh is about to swoop through it.

Walking into the side of a car or sled just bounces her back. It's getting hit head-on that flattens her.

Checkpoints along the way warm her up, and she starts there again if she gets flattened. Grab her reading glasses, fruitcake and present for a bonus. Eggnog doubles your points, and every glass after that adds more (×3, ×4…) until she gets flattened, but she gets wobbly. Watch out for presents falling out of the sky!
